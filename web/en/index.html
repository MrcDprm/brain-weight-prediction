How it works

Five models were tested on 50 different splits of the same data. The more complex ones lowered the error by less than 1 g. That is within the measurement noise, so the simplest model was chosen.

See the model comparison

Data and limits

The model is for adults only. In children and babies the relation between brain and head is different.

This is not a medical tool and must not be used for diagnosis or treatment.
